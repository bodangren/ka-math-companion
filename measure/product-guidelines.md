# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style & Tone
- **Educational & Encouraging**: Use clear, direct language that empowers learners without being patronizing.
- **Pedagogy-First**: Prioritize conceptual clarity and mathematical accuracy in all descriptions.
- **Sentence Case**: Use Sentence case for body text, CTAs, and labels.
- **Title Case**: Use Title Case for H1/H2 headers (as per project conventions).

## Visual Identity & UX
- **Clean & Accessible**: Maintain high contrast and clear visual hierarchies.
- **Material Design Principles**: Use established Material Design patterns for components and layouts.
- **Mathematical Clarity**: Ensure KaTeX notation and interactive graphs are legible and screen-reader accessible.
- **Consistent Feedback**: Provide immediate, misconception-specific feedback for all learner interactions.

## Design Tokens
- **Typography**: System fonts with fallbacks; monospace for mathematical expressions where appropriate.
- **Spacing**: Consistent 4px or 8px grid-based spacing.
- **Colors**: Use a palette that differentiates between "Conceptual Exploration", "Practice", and "Review" states.
